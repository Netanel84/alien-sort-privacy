Privacy Policy - Alien Sort

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Developer: Netanel

App package: com.netanel.aliensort

This Privacy Policy explains how Alien Sort handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information We Collect

Alien Sort does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app stores game progress locally on your device, including level progress, coins, settings, daily bonus state, and power-up inventory. This local data is used only to operate game features.

Alien Sort uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive information such as device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other technical information needed to show, measure, and protect ads.

How Information Is Used

  • Run game features and save local progress.
  • Show ads and rewarded ad offers.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Diagnose technical issues related to ads or app performance.

Sharing

Alien Sort does not sell personal information. Advertising-related data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners through Google AdMob for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention And Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, or reset your progress if the app provides that option.

Advertising-related data handled by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. For privacy requests, use the developer contact email listed on the app's Google Play store listing.

Children

Alien Sort is not intended to be directed to children under 13. If the Google Play target audience is changed to include children, this policy and the app's ad and consent configuration must be updated to comply with Google Play Families policies.

Security

The app does not maintain a user account system or a developer-operated user database. Local game data is stored on the user's device. Advertising SDK communication is handled by Google Mobile Ads over secure connections where supported.

International Users And Consent

If Alien Sort is made available in regions that require ad consent, such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app should show the required consent flow before serving personalized ads.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be available at the privacy policy URL listed in Google Play.
