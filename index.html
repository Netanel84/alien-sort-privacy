Privacy Policy - Alien Sort

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Developer: Netanel

App package: com.netanel.aliensort

This Privacy Policy explains how Alien Sort handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information We Collect

Alien Sort does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app stores game progress locally on your device, including level progress, coins, settings, daily and weekly reward state, and power-up inventory. This local data is used only to operate game features.

Alien Sort uses Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay and technical usage, such as app opens, screen views, sessions, level starts, level completions, ad reward flows, and other in-game events. Firebase Analytics may collect or receive app interactions, diagnostics, device or app instance identifiers, and approximate location derived from network information.

Alien Sort uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive information such as device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other technical information needed to show, measure, and protect ads.

How Information Is Used

  • Run game features and save local progress.
  • Understand gameplay flow and improve the app.
  • Show ads and rewarded ad offers.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Diagnose technical issues related to ads, analytics, or app performance.

Sharing

Alien Sort does not sell personal information. Analytics and advertising-related data may be shared with Google through Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK for measurement, advertising, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention And Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, or reset your progress if the app provides that option.

Analytics and advertising-related data handled by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. For privacy requests, use the developer contact email listed on the app's Google Play store listing.

Children

Alien Sort is not intended to be directed to children under 13. If the Google Play target audience is changed to include children, this policy and the app's ad and consent configuration must be updated to comply with Google Play Families policies.

Security

The app does not maintain a user account system or a developer-operated user database. Local game data remains on the device. Communication used by Google SDKs is handled through secure connections when supported by those services.

Consent And Regions

If Alien Sort is distributed in regions that require user consent for ads or analytics, the app may need to show a consent flow before using personalized ads or related processing.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version should be available at the privacy policy URL listed in Google Play.
