Privacy Policy - Alien Tube Sort

Last updated: September 29, 2026.

Developer: Netanel David Bezalel, publishing as LAST PINT STUDIO.

App: Alien Tube Sort (previously Alien Sort). Main package: com.netanel.aliensort; separate test package: com.netanel.aliensort.test.

This Privacy Policy explains how Alien Tube Sort handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information We Collect

Alien Tube Sort does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app stores game progress locally on your device, including level progress, coins, settings, daily and weekly reward state, and power-up inventory. This local data is used only to operate game features.

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics. Depending on the installed version and settings, Firebase may receive app sessions, screen views, level starts and completions, reward events, app-instance identifiers and technical information, and may derive approximate location from network information.

Version differences: the 0.3.22 test build introduced optional usage statistics, disabled by default until allowed in Settings, Privacy Choices, Usage statistics. That choice can be changed there later, and Firebase advertising storage and advertising personalization are disabled in that build. Older builds, including 0.3.21, do not provide the same optional-statistics control. Installing a main-store update does not update the separate test app. Do not assume the newer controls are available in an older installation.

Advertising uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource advertising services for banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Unity Ad Quality is included for ad-quality monitoring. Advertising services and their partners may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and other technical information to deliver and measure ads, monitor quality and prevent fraud. Google's User Messaging Platform manages the configured advertising consent form.

Earlier versions used Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Those older installations may continue to process advertising data through Google rather than the current Unity integration. Provider processing depends on the version, available ads, region and applicable settings.

How Information Is Used

  • Run game features and save local progress.
  • Understand gameplay flow and improve the app.
  • Show ads and rewarded ad offers.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Diagnose technical issues related to ads, analytics, or app performance.

Sharing

Analytics information is processed by Google Firebase as described above; builds offering the optional-statistics control require your opt-in for this collection. Advertising information may be processed by Unity, including ironSource, and advertising partners, or by Google in older advertising builds. The optional usage-statistics choice is separate from advertising consent.

Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Unity's practices are described in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Data Retention And Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, or reset your progress if the app provides that option.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local progress; it does not itself delete information held by advertising or analytics providers. Provider retention and privacy-request procedures are described in their policies. Contact lastpint.studio@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Do not send passwords or financial information.

Children

Alien Tube Sort is intended for players aged 13 and older, not for children under 13. Contact the developer if you believe information from a child under 13 was provided through the app.

Security

The app does not maintain a player account system. Progress and local reward records are stored on the device. Third-party data processing is subject to the providers' security practices; no absolute security guarantee is made.

Consent And Regions

Current Unity-based builds request advertising consent information at startup and use the consent platform's eligibility result before initializing advertising. Available choices depend on your region, the installed version, the service and its configured form. A missing form does not mean you have agreed to personalized advertising. In builds with Privacy Choices, Settings provides access to this policy, available advertising choices and optional usage statistics. Contact the developer if a form cannot be opened or a choice cannot be applied.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version should be available at the privacy policy URL listed in Google Play.
